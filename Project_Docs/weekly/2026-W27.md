## Blockers / Questions
- Calibration Issue: If the arm is not completely relaxed when clicking the calibration button, the system records a resting baseline that is too high. This makes the active muscle signals seem too small for the model to recognise, causing the system to get stuck in the "Open Hand" state.
- Camera Position and Lighting Limitations: Issues with the camera angle and lighting during testing resulted in very poor data quality when recording the "Pinch" gesture. Because of this, the trained model can recognise all gestures except the pinch action. Future work will involve adjusting the camera setup and light source, or finding a new gesture to replace it.
- Sensor Position Shifting: If the armband is taken off and put back on, even a slight change in its rotation or tightness causes the sensors to misalign with the muscles. While big movements like "Fist" still work, the system struggles to recognise specific movements like "Wrist Up" because the sensors are no longer sitting over the correct muscle. To fix this, I will either mark the exact position on my arm or record extra training data with the armband slightly twisted so the model learns to handle it.
